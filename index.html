Your information

Privacy Policy

When you contact DowMan Properties or request an inspection, we collect the details you provide, such as your name, email, phone number, property of interest, and preferred appointment time. We use these details to respond to your enquiry and coordinate property viewings.

Enquiry and inspection forms are handled by Formspree on our behalf. Do not include sensitive personal or financial information in a message. We do not sell your contact details. To ask us to update or delete information you have submitted, email hello@dowman.ng.

Property information and availability can change; please confirm details directly with our team.
